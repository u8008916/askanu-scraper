Program Overview

The Bachelor of Accounting provides students with a sound understanding of accounting, finance, commercial law and taxation. The degree satisfies the educational entrance requirements for the major professional accounting bodies in Australia.

Learning Outcomes

  • Evaluate and apply contemporary accounting and financial theories and practices.
  • Critically analyze financial statements and reports to inform business decisions.
  • Demonstrate ethical, legal, and professional conduct in business environments.
