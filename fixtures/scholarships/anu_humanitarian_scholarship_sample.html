About this Scholarship

The ANU Humanitarian Scholarship supports students from humanitarian or refugee backgrounds. It is awarded based on equity and financial need to eligible students commencing undergraduate study at ANU.

Eligibility

  • Australian citizens, permanent residents, or holders of a humanitarian visa
  • Commencing undergraduate students
  • Demonstrated financial need
