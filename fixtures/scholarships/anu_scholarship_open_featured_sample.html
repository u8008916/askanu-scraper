ANU International Achievement Award

About this scholarship

The award attracts academically capable overseas students to eligible ANU coursework programs.

Field of study

Any eligible program except excluded programs listed by ANU

Eligibility

Scholarship type

Academic

Student type

International

Study level

Undergraduate/Bachelor, Honours, Postgraduate/Masters, and Graduate certificate

Selection bases

Academic merit

The award is available to eligible commencing or continuing international students.

Application period

Open for applications

Application period is open all year round.

Value

20%, 25% or 50% off tuition fees for up to 4 years.

Application requirement

No application is required for this scholarship as all eligible candidates will be considered.
