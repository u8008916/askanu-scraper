Alex Rodgers Travel Grant

About this scholarship

The grant assists an eligible astronomy and astrophysics student with relevant travel.

Field of study

Astronomy and Astrophysics

Eligibility

Scholarship type

Grant/Bursary

Student type

Domestic, International

Study level

Postgraduate research (HDR)

Selection bases

Academic merit

Applicants must be full-time current RSAA PhD students.

Application period

Open for applications

Application period is open all year round.

Value

$5,000 per annum. One off payment.

Application requirement

An application is required for this scholarship.
